# Greenscape Pro — AI Opportunity Ranking

Greenscape Pro is a premium Phoenix residential hardscape/landscape design-build
company. The pairing below is based on the business's actual constraint
analysis, not on feature novelty. All dollar figures are estimates from the
owner and should be validated before committing build effort.

---

## Ranked AI Opportunities

### 1. AI Proposal & Quote Copilot

**Purpose:** Convert an unstructured site-walk into a priced, professional
proposal draft in minutes instead of 6–9 days.

**What it does:**
- Reads unstructured site-walk notes and produces a structured project scope.
- Matches requested work to the pricing catalog and computes prices
  deterministically in code.
- Drafts a professional proposal with assumptions, questions, and risk flags.
- Keeps a human approval step before anything is sent.

**Replaces / unblocks:** The founder's manual interpretation, spreadsheet
pricing, and proposal drafting — the 6–9 day conversion bottleneck.

**Estimated ROI:** Protects a material share of the 35–40% of qualified
opportunities currently lost to faster competitors. Turning days into minutes
on the core revenue step has the highest leverage-per-hour of engineering.

**Why it's #1:** Greenscape is **quote-constrained, not lead-constrained.** The
company spends ~$25K–$30K/month on acquisition and Meta performance is already
healthy. But proposals take 6–9 days and 35–40% of qualified opportunities are
lost to faster competitors. Adding more leads before fixing proposal throughput
would amplify the existing bottleneck — the highest-leverage move is to remove
the conversion bottleneck itself.

---

### 2. Post-Sign Project Unblocker

**Purpose:** Proactively identify and resolve blockers (permits, HOA
conditions, material lead times, scheduling) after a proposal is signed.

**What it does:**
- Tracks post-sign tasks against deadlines and flags overdue items.
- Drafts follow-up updates and nudges to keep the build moving.
- Surfaces supply-chain and scheduling risks early.

**Replaces / unblocks:** Manual chasing and the hidden cost of projects stalling
after signature.

**Estimated ROI:** Meaningful — protects already-won revenue that could be
eroded by delays and improves on-time delivery reputation.

**Why this priority:** The sale is won; the risk is downstream revenue and
customer satisfaction. Still lower leverage than the proposal cycle, because
quote speed is the primary constraint.

---

### 3. Customer Project Update Agent

**Purpose:** Keep customers informed with automated, warm project updates.

**What it does:**
- Drafts periodic progress updates from job notes.
- Answers routine "when will it be done?" questions.
- Reduces inbound status-check calls.

**Replaces / unblocks:** The team's time spent on repetitive status
communication; improves the premium customer experience.

**Estimated ROI:** Time savings on communication plus improved retention and
referrals. Moderate and recurring.

**Why this priority:** High value but operates after the proposal is won, so it
doesn't move the conversion bottleneck.

---

### 4. Closed-Lost Lead Reactivation Agent

**Purpose:** Nurture and reactivate leads that went with a competitor or went
quiet.

**What it does:**
- Segments closed-lost and dormant leads.
- Sends personalized, value-driven re-engagement at the right cadence.
- Surfaces warm signals to the team for a human touch.

**Replaces / unblocks:** Lost revenue from the 35–40% of qualified leads that
this business already loses today.

**Estimated ROI:** Opportune — recovers a share of already-qualified demand with
relatively low spend.

**Why this priority:** Directly recovers conversion losses, but it assumes the
proposal pipeline itself is healthy. Worth doing only after fixing quote
throughput.

---

### 5. AI Lead Qualification Agent

**Purpose:** Qualify inbound leads before sales effort is spent.

**What it does:**
- Triages inquiries for budget, scope, and timeline fit.
- Routes the best leads to the team.
- Drafts first-response replies.

**Replaces / unblocks:** Time spent qualifying low-value inbound.

**Estimated ROI:** Small — documented direct savings are approximately
**1–2 hours/week**.

**Why this priority:** Lead qualification is genuinely useful, but its documented
savings are only about 1–2 hours/week. That is far less leverage than the quote
cycle, post-sign delays, customer communication, and closed-lost reactivation,
so it ranks last.

---

## Why is #1 the #1 — and not simply because the founder said so?

The ranking follows the business data, not the founder's preference. The
evidence is the conversion bottleneck and quantified revenue risk: healthy
acquisition spend (~$25K–$30K/month), a multi-day manual proposal cycle, and an
estimated 35–40% loss of qualified opportunities to faster competitors. Those
numbers make proposal throughput the binding constraint, so the copilot ranks
first independently.

In this case the founder's instinct happens to align with the data — but had the
numbers pointed elsewhere (e.g., weak acquisition), the ranking would have led
with lead generation instead. We are not blindly following his preference; we
are following the conversion bottleneck and the quantified revenue risk it
represents.

---

## One agent considered but excluded

**AI Crew Coaching and Upsell Assistant** — excluded from the top five.

It addresses a real problem and could theoretically protect roughly **$104K/year**
in mistakes or missed upsells, but it operates at a smaller scale than the
quote-cycle bottleneck and the other selected interventions. It should be
revisited after the higher-leverage revenue and operational constraints are
addressed.

We also explicitly **reject a marketing/content agent** from the top five:
lead volume is not currently the constraint, so generating more content would
amplify the existing pipeline bottleneck rather than relieve it.
